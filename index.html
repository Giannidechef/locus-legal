Support

Locus blocks the apps you choose until you leave the place where you started, or until you scan your unlock card.

Common questions

My apps didn't unlock when I left. Check that Location is set to Always for Locus (Settings › Locus › Location). Unlocking happens when you are past the radius you chose; indoors, GPS can take a minute.

I lost my unlock card. Walk out of the place: the session ends on its own. Then make a new code in Settings › Unlock code. Locus never stores the code itself, so it can't show it to you again.

A minimum time is set and I left early. Your apps unlock when the minimum time is up, or when you come back.

Restoring Locus Pro. Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
